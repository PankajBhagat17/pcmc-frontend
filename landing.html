Intelligent Issue Routing

Our AI engine understands your concern, categorizes it accurately, and dispatches it to the responsible department instantly—bypassing traditional red tape.

🎙️

1. Voice or Text Input

Describe your issue naturally. Use English, Marathi, or Hindi. Speak or type it; our system listens and comprehends context.

🤖

2. AI Categorization

Advanced Natural Language Processing extracts key entities, identifying the exact issue (e.g., 'pothole', 'street light') and exact location.

⚡

3. Automated Routing

The categorized ticket is instantly dispatched directly to the dashboard of the responsible ward officer, triggering SLA timers.
